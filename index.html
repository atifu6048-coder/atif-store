The Transparency Manifesto

We do not participate in fast consumer cycles. Atelier & Co. was established on the conviction that clothing worn close to human bodies should be pure, uncompromised, and resilient enough to be passed to future generations.

Zero Azo Dye Certification

All colorations across our Autumn Neutrality capsule are derived from unbleached natural animal hairs and plant-based bath infusions in northern Portugal.

Family Longevity Protocol

If a toddler romper outgrows your youngest child, return it to any flagship. We professionally comb, repair, and re-allocate it through our Archival Resale program.
